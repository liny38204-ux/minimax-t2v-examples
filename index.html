正例设计

已完成 10.12 秒
原始 prompt 
10秒短片，横屏16:9，2个镜头硬切，全片统一为日系水彩动画风格。镜头1：少女在樱花树下回头微笑。镜头2：同一少女在樱花树下低头看手机，画风保持水彩不变。
PE 后 prompt 
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Watercolor-animated, a medium shot at eye level as the camera pushes in at slow speed toward a teenage girl standing beneath a blooming cherry blossom tree. She has chin-length dark brown hair with soft bangs tucked behind one ear, styled with a pale blue ribbon, and wears a classic navy-and-white sailor school uniform with a pleated skirt rendered in soft watercolor bleeds on textured paper. Positioned slightly to the left of center, her back is initially angled toward the viewer as translucent pink and white cherry petals drift diagonally through the frame. Dappled spring sunlight filters through the overhanging branches above, casting soft, layered pastel shadows on her shoulders and the pale stone path beneath her feet. In a fluid motion, she gently rotates her torso and turns her head over her shoulder, her hair shifting in the spring breeze, and her lips part into a warm, gentle smile with soft pink blush washes blooming on her cheeks as she blinks softly. [Shot 2] At 00:05.200, the camera cuts to a medium close-up from a high-three-quarter profile angle with a static shot framing the teenage girl from Shot 1. She now tilts her head down toward a modern silver smartphone cradled in both hands in the lower center foreground. The watercolor textures remain consistent, with visible pigment pooling along the edges of the hand-painted cherry branches that frame the upper edge of the composition. Her slender thumbs make light scrolling motions across the screen, her dark eyelashes casting delicate painted shadows on her cheeks, while a single stray petal flutters past her fingers and settles briefly on her sleeve before sliding away.
overall_soundscape: A gentle spring breeze rustles audibly through the leafy cherry blossom canopy, accompanied by the distinct soft fluttering of drifting petals. Fabric rustles distinctly as the girl turns her shoulders, followed by the pronounced light tap and faint glide of a thumb contacting the glass smartphone screen in the foreground, with distant, faint chirping of sparrows audible throughout.
non_diegetic_music: A delicate acoustic nylon-string guitar and warm solo piano playing a gentle arpeggiated melody at a slow, reflective tempo, maintaining a steady, quiet dynamic with no sudden swells.
